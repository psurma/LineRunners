Parkland Walk

London's longest nature reserve along the Edgware, Highgate & Alexandra Palace railway (closed 1954) — old platforms at Crouch End, the Highgate tunnel portals, then over St James Lane viaduct to Ally Pally.

  • TypeDisused railway
  • Distance4.5 mi (7.3 km)
  • RouteFinsbury Park → Alexandra Palace (the old GNR branch)
  • StartFinsbury Park (Victoria/Piccadilly)
  • ShapePoint to point
See it on the map

Who it suits

Leafy, car-free and gently graded, with one road link over Highgate Hill; train home from Alexandra Palace.
