Hackney Half

May's flat, flowing closed-road half through the heart of Hackney: Homerton to Hackney Downs, the Town Hall and Empire on Mare Street, Dalston and Kingsland High Street, London Fields and Broadway Market, a Haggerston loop into Victoria Park around halfway, then Hackney Wick's canalside warehouses and the Olympic Park fringe back to the Marshes.

  • Distance13.1 mi (21.1 km)
  • RouteHackney Marshes → Hackney Central → Dalston → London Fields → Victoria Park → Hackney Wick → Hackney Marshes
  • StartHackney Wick / Homerton (Hackney Marshes festival village)
  • ShapeLoop — finishes where it starts

Who it suits

The classic East London half — barely a bump all the way round, with Overground stations never far. Indicative line traced from the course narrative.
