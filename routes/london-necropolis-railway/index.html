London Necropolis Railway

The funeral railway (1854–1941) left its private terminus on Westminster Bridge Road, beside Waterloo, ran the LSWR main line to Brookwood, then reversed down a cemetery branch to North station (Nonconformists) and South station (Anglicans). The terminus was bombed in 1941; the branch was lifted by 1948.

Who it suits

A long point-to-point on the South Western pavement, then cemetery roads to South station. Flat, and a full day — trains home from Brookwood. The live railway is not the path, and North station sits off in the trees.
