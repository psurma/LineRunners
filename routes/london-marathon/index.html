London Marathon

The whole iconic course: from the Blackheath starts out past Woolwich, down to the Cutty Sark, round Rotherhithe and over Tower Bridge, the long loop through the Isle of Dogs and Canary Wharf, then back past the Tower and along the Embankment through Westminster to finish on The Mall.

  • TypeRace route
  • Distance26.2 mi (42.2 km)
  • RouteBlackheath → Cutty Sark → Tower Bridge → Canary Wharf → The Mall
  • StartBlackheath rail / Greenwich Park
  • ShapePoint to point

Who it suits

A full marathon on flat, closed roads — our longest route by far, with rail and DLR bail-out points all along it. Indicative line traced from the course map.
