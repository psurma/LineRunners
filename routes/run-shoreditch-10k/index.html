Run Shoreditch 10K

The 2025 closed-road course, street for street from the official route map: Bridport Place over the Regent's Canal, Rotherfield Street and Ecclesbourne Road, the long Southgate Road climb to Newington Green, the Matthias–Boleyn–Mildmay loop at the top, back through De Beauvoir's grid — Tottenham, Buckingham, Stamford, round De Beauvoir Square — then Downham Road, Hertford Road and Whitmore Road over the canal to Hoxton Street, finishing on Mintern Street by Shoreditch Park.
