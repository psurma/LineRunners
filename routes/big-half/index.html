The Big Half

London's great half-marathon route: east along The Highway past Wapping to loop Canary Wharf, back over Tower Bridge, then the south bank through Bermondsey, Rotherhithe and Deptford to finish at the Cutty Sark.

  • TypeRace route
  • Distance13.1 mi (21.1 km)
  • RouteTower Bridge → Canary Wharf → Greenwich
  • StartTower Gateway (DLR)
  • ShapePoint to point

Who it suits

A full half-marathon on flat, iconic riverside roads; point-to-point with the DLR home from Greenwich. Indicative line traced from the 2022 course map.
