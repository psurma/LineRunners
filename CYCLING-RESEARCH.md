# London Cycling — Sites, Clubs & Routes to Explore

A curated directory of good London cycling websites — route/path planners and cycle
clubs — to explore. All links verified when compiled (July 2026). There's a natural
tie-in to TubeRun: a lot of these ride the same parks, canals and river paths.

## Plan routes & find cycle paths

- **[TfL Cycling — routes & maps](https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/cycling/routes-and-maps)** — the official **Cycleways** network (the segregated routes that replaced Cycle Superhighways/Quietways), plus cycle route-finding in the **TfL Go** app.
- **[Sustrans — National Cycle Network](https://www.sustrans.org.uk/)** — traffic-free and signed routes across London (e.g. the Thames, canal and Lea Valley paths).
- Route-planning tools worth a look: **[Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/)**, **[Strava](https://www.strava.com/)** (route builder + local segment/club discovery), and **[cycle.travel](https://cycle.travel/)** (quiet-route planner).

## Clubs & group rides

- **[London Dynamo](https://www.londondynamo.co.uk/)** — big West London club (Richmond Park), racing/coaching, plus [club rides](https://www.londondynamo.co.uk/club-rides/) and newcomer rides.
- **[Islington Cycling Club](https://islington.cc/)** — London's largest (~900 members), friendly/diverse North London, twice-monthly intro rides with coffee & cake.
- **[Dulwich Paragon](https://www.dulwichparagon.com/)** — SE London institution (since 1935), Saturday runs from Herne Hill Velodrome into the Kent/Surrey lanes.
- **[Regent's Park Cyclists](https://www.regentsparkcyclists.com/)** — the hub for the famous early-morning Regent's Park loop scene (30+ clubs represented).
- **Rapha Cycling Club (RCC)** — social/clubhouse rides via **[rapha.cc](https://www.rapha.cc/)**; plus community crews like **Chain Gang Cyclists** (Regent's Park laps + Rapha coffee, Richmond Park brunch rides for newcomers).

## Women / trans / beginner-friendly

- **[Velociposse](https://www.velociposse.cc/)** — women, trans & non-binary; relaxed no-drop rides and Regent's/Richmond Park intro sessions.
- **[London Dynamo women's club](https://www.londondynamo.co.uk/womens-cycling-club/)** — women-only rides 2nd Saturday monthly + open days.
- **Bella Velo** (SW London women's club) — and this **[women's clubs roundup](https://sundaysinsurance.co.uk/blog/our-favourite-women-s-cycling-clubs-in-london)** lists more.

## Campaign & community (great for local paths + organised rides)

- **[London Cycling Campaign](https://lcc.org.uk/)** — 20k supporters, a group in **all 32 boroughs**, and a busy [rides & events calendar](https://lcc.org.uk/events-calendar/). Borough examples: [Kingston](https://kingstoncycling.org.uk/), [Barnet](https://barnetlcc.org/), [Greenwich](https://greenwichcycle.wordpress.com/), [Southwark](https://southwarkcyclists.org.uk/).
- **[Cycling UK](https://www.cyclinguk.org/)** — national body with London local groups, route listings and events.
- Handy overview to browse: **[Stolt's "Best Social Cycling Clubs in London"](https://stoltrunning.com/the-best-social-cycling-clubs-in-london-2024-guide/)**.

## Iconic London riding spots

Good places to build routes around:

- **Regent's Park** outer circle
- **Richmond Park** (road loop + the off-road Tamsin Trail)
- **Lee Valley VeloPark** (Olympic velodrome + road circuit)
- **Herne Hill Velodrome**
- **Regent's Canal / Grand Union** towpaths
